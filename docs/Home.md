## What is Death Outcomes?

**Death Outcomes** is a lightweight, server-side Minecraft mod designed to add engaging, customizable consequences to player deaths. Whether you operate a competitive PvP SMP, a hardcore survival server, or a casual multiplayer realm, Death Outcomes provides meaningful rewards and tactical tracking tools:

## Highlights

* **100% Server-Side**: Players can join with standard vanilla clients without needing to download any mod or resource pack.
* **Reliable PvP Detection**: Supports both direct sword/axe hits and indirect kills via arrows, tridents, fireworks, splash potions, and magical damage.
* **Persistent Offline Tracking**: Tracks players even after they disconnect by storing their last known coordinates and dimension in world data.
